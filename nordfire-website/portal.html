How It Works

From Application to Active Account

A structured three-step process ensures every trade account is properly vetted and set up with the right pricing band before access is granted.

01
Complete the Credit Application

Fill in the online trade account application with your business name, registered address, Companies House number, annual turnover, and two trade references. Review and sign the NORD Fire Terms & Conditions digitally before submitting.

Business Details Trade References Digital Signature T&Cs Agreement
02
Dual-Approval Review

Your assigned NORD Sales Representative reviews the application and routes it to a Director for final approval via a secure, single-use approval link. The Director sets your pricing band (A–D) and credit limit. You receive your login credentials by email once approved.

Rep Review Director Sign-Off Band Assignment Credit Limit Set
03
Browse, Quote & Order

Log in to the trade portal to access the full catalogue at your assigned band price. Build quotes, export branded PDFs, convert quotes to orders, and manage your account. Your client portal gives you real-time visibility of orders, payments, and outstanding balance.

Pricing Structure

Tiered Pricing Bands

Pricing bands are assigned during the approval process and applied server-side to every product in the catalogue. Your band is always accurate — no manual calculations required.

Band Account Type Typical Discount Credit Terms Suitable For
Band A Premium Trade Maximum discount tier 30–60 day credit High-volume contractors, national accounts
Band B Established Trade Mid-high discount 30 day credit Regional contractors, systems integrators
Band C Standard Trade Standard trade discount 30 day credit / proforma Facilities managers, smaller contractors
Band D New Account Entry-level trade discount Proforma / card New trade customers, sole traders

Exact discount percentages are commercially sensitive and communicated directly during the approval process. Band upgrades are reviewed annually or on request.

Common Questions

Trade Account FAQs

Trade accounts are available to fire safety contractors, systems engineers, facilities managers, electrical contractors, and distributors or wholesalers operating in the UK. Sole traders and limited companies are both eligible to apply.
Most applications are reviewed by a NORD Sales Representative within one business day and routed to a Director for final approval. You will typically receive your login credentials within 2–3 business days of submitting a complete application.
Your pricing band (A–D) is assigned by a NORD Director during the approval process, based on your business type, expected order volume, and trading history. Band upgrades can be requested at any time and are reviewed annually as standard.
Yes. NORD Sales Representatives and Directors can access client accounts within the portal to build quotes, manage orders, and update account details on behalf of clients. Clients also have their own login to the client-facing portal.
Accepted quotes can be converted to orders directly within the portal. A NORD team member will confirm the order, update the status, and add tracking events as the order progresses through to dispatch and delivery. The client receives email notifications at each stage.
Payments are recorded by NORD Directors within the admin portal against the relevant client account. Clients can view their full payment history, outstanding balance, and credit utilisation in real time through their client portal dashboard.
