01
Service Standards & SLA Commitments
All services shall be delivered by BAFE-registered, NSI Gold-approved engineers to the applicable British Standards listed in the Schedule of Rates. Core Fire Protection commits to a maximum 4-hour emergency response time and next-business-day attendance for priority faults. All PPM visits are conducted in accordance with the latest edition of the relevant standard, including BS 5839-1:2025. Service reports will be issued within 5 working days of each visit. Engineers will carry all appropriate certification and authorisation for the systems they maintain.
02
Access & Cooperation Requirements
The Client agrees to provide reasonable access to all equipment and systems during normal business hours (08:00–17:00 Monday–Friday, excluding bank holidays). Service visits will be scheduled with a minimum of 5 working days' notice. Failed access due to client unavailability may result in a re-attendance charge. The Client shall ensure that a suitably authorised person is available to accompany the engineer and sign off the service report on completion. Emergency call-outs are available 24/7, 365 days, at the rates specified in the Schedule.
03
Pricing, Invoicing & Payment Terms
All prices are exclusive of VAT at the prevailing UK rate (currently 20%). Invoices are raised per completed visit — no advance payment is required. Payment is due within the agreed terms from the invoice date. Annual price adjustments are applied at the agreed escalation rate, with 30 days written notice. Late payment may incur statutory interest under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998. No hidden call-out charges apply for routine PPM visits. Minor remedial works under £250 are rectified on the same visit at no additional charge.
04
Remedial Works & Defect Rectification
Minor defects identified during PPM visits (faulty detectors, discharged batteries, failed indicators) are rectified on the same visit at no additional call-out charge. Remedial works above £250 will always be quoted in writing and require written client approval before proceeding. Critical life-safety defects will be communicated to the responsible person immediately. A remedial works record will be maintained and referenced in all subsequent service reports. Core Fire accepts no liability for system failure resulting from defects not addressed within a reasonable timescale following written recommendation.
05
Liability, Indemnity & Insurance
Core Fire Protection maintains a minimum of £5 million public liability insurance and £2 million professional indemnity insurance. Liability for any single incident is limited to the total annual contract value. Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or economic loss. The Client remains the Responsible Person under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 and is responsible for ensuring the fire strategy remains appropriate for their premises. Core Fire accepts no liability for pre-existing defects not identified during the agreed scope of service.
06
Intellectual Property & Documentation
All service reports, certificates, drawings, and documentation produced by Core Fire remain the intellectual property of Core Fire until all invoices are settled in full. Upon settlement, the Client receives full rights to the documentation for the purposes of compliance and insurance. This Service Agreement, Schedule of Rates, and associated documents are confidential and are not to be shared with third parties without prior written consent.
07
Data Protection & UK GDPR Compliance
Core Fire Protection processes personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Personal data collected under this agreement (names, contact details, site information) is used solely for the purpose of delivering contracted services and maintaining compliance records. Data will not be shared with third parties except where required by law. Contact details are held for the duration of the contract plus 7 years for audit purposes. The Client has the right to access, correct, or request deletion of their data at any time.
08
Force Majeure & Business Continuity
Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform obligations where such failure results from circumstances beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, pandemic, civil unrest, or government restriction. Core Fire will notify the Client within 24 hours of any force majeure event affecting service delivery. Core Fire maintains a business continuity plan to ensure critical life-safety systems are prioritised during recovery. Any delayed PPM visits due to force majeure will be rescheduled at no additional cost to the Client.
09
Contract Renewal, Variation & Termination
Fixed-term agreements require 90 days written notice before expiry to terminate without penalty. Rolling contracts may be terminated with 30 days written notice by either party. No automatic multi-year lock-in applies — renewal is always an active choice. Variations to the Schedule of Services or Rates require written agreement from both parties. Either party may terminate immediately in the event of material breach, subject to a 14-day cure period. Core Fire reserves the right to suspend services where invoices are outstanding beyond 60 days from due date.
10
Governing Law & Dispute Resolution
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales (or Scots law where the site is in Scotland). Any dispute arising from this agreement shall first be subject to good-faith negotiation between nominated representatives. If unresolved within 30 days, the parties agree to attempt mediation via a mutually agreed independent mediator before resorting to litigation. The courts of England and Wales (or Scotland, as applicable) shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any disputes not resolved by alternative means.
